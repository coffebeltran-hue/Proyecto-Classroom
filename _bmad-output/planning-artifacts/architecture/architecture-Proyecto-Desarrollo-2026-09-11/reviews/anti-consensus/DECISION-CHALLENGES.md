## Avoid unnecessary redesign

No reviewer evidence justifies replacing the stack, adding another broker or introducing microservices. Receipt sequence can be allocated under the existing intake lock. Capacity recovery can use one coalesced existing outbox operation per quota account plus a bounded sweep. Purge correctness needs an ordered journal and shared eligibility fence, not a general distributed transaction framework. Manually versioned templates are adequate if their freeze and verification work.

Keep future authoritative grading at a documented boundary; no evaluator registry/plugin engine or student execution service belongs in this MVP merely to demonstrate future extensibility. Keep distinct academic evidence concepts: collapsing them would violate adopted semantics rather than simplify safely.
